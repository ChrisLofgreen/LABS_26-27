
# Strange animal simulator

This program simulates some probable aspects of animal behavior in a zoo environment with three species of animal: Beavers, Orangutans and Tyrannosaurs.
The behaiviors can be followed as an observer as the program provides small reports for every simulated day.
A more substantial report is deliviered every 15 days.

The simulation divides the day in to 4 different parts where animals can behave differently, this is to simulate morning, midday, evening and night.

Environment includes walls to keep the animals in, and forests for them to live in, and also a stream in the beaver-pen
and a drop of point for supplamentary tyrannosaur food in the tyrannosaur pen.

## The program simulates

#### Beavers 

Finding a stream and building a dam, and the resulting flooding. 

#### Orangutans

Eating fruit and migrating or moving out if there is no fruit in the forest.

#### Tyrannosaurs

Lumbering around, resting, destroying walls and moving out or eating supplied food and other animals.

run main.py
